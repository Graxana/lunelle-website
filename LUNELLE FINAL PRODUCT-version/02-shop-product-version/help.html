Orders and availability

When you send an order enquiry, LUNELLE should confirm stock, shade or size, final price, delivery availability and estimated delivery time before you pay. An enquiry sent through WhatsApp is not an order confirmation by itself.

Payments and safety

This demo does not collect online payments. Ask the store for its verified payment details after your order is confirmed. Never share your mobile-money PIN, bank password or one-time security code.

Returns and product concerns

Ask LUNELLE about its actual return, exchange and damaged-item policy before placing an order. For cosmetics, check the packaging, ingredients, expiry information and product directions. Do not use a product if its seal or packaging appears tampered with.
